# How to teach the estimator from Super Admin

Use `/admin/knowledge` to publish reviewed global estimator lessons. Good lessons describe a repair scenario, the operations that should be considered, and what must remain TBC or inspection-only.

Example:

Title: Rear impact — complete estimate checklist

Content: When the damage is at the right rear, review bumper cover, bumper retainers, lower garnish, lamps, sensors/loom, inner trims, wheel-arch interfaces, tailgate fittings/badges, decals/livery, alignment/gap checks, adjacent blend panels and check-after-strip items such as reinforcement, absorber, lamp mounting panel and rear body panel. Create one estimate line per operation. Replacement parts remain TBC until supplier pricing is available.

The learning queue should be used to approve/reject recurring estimator corrections before they become global knowledge.
