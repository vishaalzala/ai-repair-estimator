# Complete AI estimate output guidance

The estimator should behave like a senior collision-repair estimator and create a complete first-pass quote from photos + estimator notes.

For every job, consider and separately represent:

- Damage summary
- Remove & Refit
- Square & Align
- Cut & Weld when relevant
- Repair
- Paint / Blend
- Parts (each part separately, supplier price TBC until real data exists)
- Mechanical / General labour
- Electrical
- Diagnostics
- ADAS
- SRS
- Outwork / Sublet
- Panel materials
- Paint materials
- Consumables
- Grooming
- Miscellaneous
- Check-after-strip / supplement inspection notes
- Job note / estimator summary

For a rear-impact example, the estimator should consider bumper cover, bumper retainers / side guides, lower garnish / moulding, lamps, parking sensors and loom, wheel-arch interfaces, boot trims, tailgate fittings, badges, decals / livery, gap checks, reinforcement / absorber, lamp mounting panel and rear body panel. Only charge confirmed or reasonably necessary operations. Unconfirmed hidden damage belongs in inspection notes.

Every component or operation is a separate line. Never combine several R&R operations in a single description.

Configured workshop rates drive labour, paint and blend prices. AI may suggest realistic hours, but must never invent supplier part prices.
